About the ZIP

Each ZIP holds numbered JPEG frames plus frames.csv (frame file, time in seconds, width, height, motion event number, motion score, and which side the motion was on: L or R) and source.json (video name, length, settings). The timestamps let you trace any frame back to its spot in the video.

In motion mode, the graph shows movement over time. The shaded bands are the parts that get saved. Change sensitivity or the before/after seconds and the bands update instantly without scanning again.

Tip: for training data, a few hundred varied frames across many throws beats thousands of near-duplicates.

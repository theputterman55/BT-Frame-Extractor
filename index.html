About the ZIP

Each ZIP holds numbered JPEG frames plus frames.csv (frame file, time in seconds, width, height) and source.json (video name, length, settings). The timestamps let you trace any frame back to its spot in the video.

Tip: for training data, a few hundred varied frames across many throws beats thousands of near-duplicates. Try 3 to 5 frames per second.
